Mnemo for macOS and Windows, free to download.

**New in 1.12.0: Mnemo speaks English.**
- **The whole interface in English or French:** Sessions, Memory, Archivist, Settings, Documentation, and the desktop app (menus, dialogs, notifications, installer).
- **Choose the language when you install:** the first step of the installer asks for Français or English, preselected on your system language.
- **Change it any time** in Settings › Settings › System › Interface › Language (System language, Français, English). The whole interface switches as soon as you save, without reloading; the app's menus follow.
- `mnemo init --langue fr|en` sets the language from a terminal.
- Not translated in this release: the content of your memory (never translated), the text Mnemo injects into sessions, the `mnemo` command, and some messages that come from the service (errors, warnings), still in French.
